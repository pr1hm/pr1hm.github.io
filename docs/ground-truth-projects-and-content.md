## 1. Project Specifications Integrated

### A. FrameRead (`pr1hm/FrameRead`)
- **Category:** `Video Intelligence`
- **Specification:** An end-to-end, 100% offline local video intelligence pipeline. Combines OpenAI Whisper for audio transcription with scene-change frame extraction and **Qwen2-VL** inference via Hugging Face. Synthesizes timestamp-accurate written briefs and answers evidence-backed queries with zero cloud calls.
- **Repository:** `https://github.com/pr1hm/FrameRead`

### B. OpenSeal (`pr1hm/openseal` & `openseal.prthm.xyz`)
- **Category:** `Document Signing`
- **Specification:** An open-source, self-hosted DocuSign alternative built on FastAPI (Python) and Vanilla JS. Enables uploading PDFs, interactive drag-and-drop bounding box placement for signature coordinates, dynamic UUID signing URLs, and cross-platform canvas drawing.
- **Working URL:** `https://openseal.prthm.xyz`
- **Repository:** `https://github.com/pr1hm/openseal`

### C. Conlatus AI (`conlatus/conlatus`)
- **Category:** `AI Proctoring`
- **Specification:** Full-stack autonomous examination and screening platform featuring conversational AI interviews, Safe Exam Browser (SEB) gate lockdown, and real-time computer vision proctoring (gaze tracking, multi-face presence, anomaly telemetry).
- **Repository:** `https://github.com/conlatus/conlatus`

### D. Best Furniture & Jeet Furniture (Bardoli, Gujarat)
- **Category:** `Commercial Web`
- **Specification:** Production web platforms, digital showrooms, and inquiry funnels built for custom furniture manufacturers in Bardoli, Gujarat.
- **Live URLs:** `https://bestfurniture.store` & `https://jeetfurniture.com`
