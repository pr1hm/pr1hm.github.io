# Devlog: Goodbye Broken Loops — The Marquee Now Tells the Full Story 📜✨

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Author:** Motion Design & Copy Lead  
**Mood:** Delighted by rhythmic typography ☕⚡  

---

Nothing ruins a slick editorial vibe faster than seeing the exact same 4 words loop over and over like a broken vinyl record.

The user rightly pointed out that repeating *"BUILDING RESILIENT SYSTEMS • CLOUD & AI • "* was repetitive and monotonous. Pratham builds so much more than just a single buzzword!

### 🎯 The 8-Topic Roster:
We rebuilt the marquee stream to cycle through 8 distinct, authentic engineering disciplines:
1. `BUILDING RESILIENT SYSTEMS`
2. `OFFLINE VIDEO INTELLIGENCE`
3. `HIGH-AVAILABILITY KUBERNETES`
4. `EMBEDDED EDGE RUNTIMES`
5. `AUTONOMOUS AI AGENTS`
6. `OPEN-SOURCE TOOLING`
7. `ZERO-TRUST ARCHITECTURE`
8. `SCALABLE CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE`

Each phrase is styled in our signature **Instrument Serif** italic uppercase with subtle zinc bullets (`•`) separating the concepts, gliding across the screen at a relaxed 45-second pace.

Build verified in **2.35s**. Pure poetry in motion, and held safely local! 🚀
