# Devlog: The Terminal `[P]` Favicon Has Landed ⚡💻

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Author:** Senior Frontend & Brand Designer  
**Mood:** Delighted by crisp vector geometry ☕🎯  

---

You know that feeling when a website is 99% gorgeous, but the browser tab still shows a purple Vite lightning bolt? Yeah, that had to go!

The user picked **Option B: The Terminal `[P]` Mark**, and we crafted a custom, retina-crisp SVG icon that looks like it belongs on an elite engineer's workstation.

### 📐 The Blueprint:
- A rounded squircle canvas in obsidian `#09090b` with a fine zinc border rim (`#27272a`).
- Muted terminal brackets `[` and `]` framing a bold, high-contrast lettermark **P** in pure `#f4f4f5`.
- Engineered with pixel-clean stroke caps and tested for instant recognition at standard 16x16, 32x32, and high-DPI bookmark views.

Rebuilt in **1.55s**, zero warnings, ready to shine in every tab! 🚀
